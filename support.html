Chronos Support

Chronos is a history app by Factory Labs.

Need help or found a bug? Email us at factorylabsteam@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and its OS version, and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Chronos works without signing up, and your progress is stored on your phone.

How do I change the language?
Open Profile, then Preferences, and choose Language. Chronos is available in English and Georgian. You can also change it in your phone settings under Chronos.

How do I switch to dark mode?
Open Profile, then Preferences, and choose Appearance.

How do I change the app icon?
Open Profile, then Preferences, and tap App icon to pick one of six icons.

How do I start over?
Open Profile, then Preferences, and tap Reset progress. This erases your XP, streak, badges and saved items, and cannot be undone.

Will my progress move to a new phone?
Progress is stored on the device, so it may not carry over to a new phone, and deleting the app erases it.

I found a mistake in a story.
Thank you! Email us the story title and what looks wrong. Every story shows whether it is a confirmed fact, a theory or a legend, and lists its sources.
