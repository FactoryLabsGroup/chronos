Inside the app

Five ways into the past

Home

Your feed never ends

Roughly two quick facts for every story, dealt so the subject keeps changing and nothing you've seen comes back while something new is waiting.

  • Swipe up for the next card
  • Tap to read the whole story
  • Bookmark anything worth keeping

Stories

Big stories, as deep as you like

Full story opens a short illustrated read: a question first, the answer after, then who, when and where, and what was happening elsewhere in the world at the same time.

  • Three depths for every explanation
  • Meanwhile, elsewhere in the world
  • Sources named for every story

Timeline

Scrub through 1.8 million years

From the first humans at Dmanisi to the first website, every event sits on one ruler. Drag to travel, pinch to zoom from eras down to single years.

  • Five eras, from the Ancient World to today
  • Pinch between eras, centuries and years
  • Smooth at up to 120 Hz

World map

Explore history place by place

59 regions and 84 civilizations on a map of the world. Tap a place to see who lived there, when, and the stories that happened there.

  • Civilizations on every continent
  • Stories tied to where they happened
  • Keep track of the regions you've explored

Streaks and levels

Make history a daily habit

Chronos remembers what you read and what you keep, and brings the best of it back. Grow from Curious visitor to Keeper of time.

  • Read each day to keep a streak
  • Levels and badges mark the way
  • Saved cards wait on your shelf

Privacy

No accounts. No ads. No tracking.

Your progress, saved cards, streak and badges stay on your phone. We collect only anonymous usage and crash information to make Chronos better, and we never sell it.
