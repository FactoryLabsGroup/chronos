Home

Roughly two quick facts for every story, dealt so the subject keeps changing and nothing you've seen comes back while something new is waiting.

Stories

Big stories, as deep as you like

Full story opens a short illustrated read: a question first, the answer after, then who, when and where, and what was happening elsewhere in the world at the same time.

  • Meanwhile, elsewhere in the world

Timeline

Honest history

Every card says how sure we are

History is full of great stories that never happened. Chronos labels each one, and names its sources.

Confirmed fact

Supported by multiple reliable sources and broadly accepted by historians.

Historical theory

The event is real, but historians still debate its causes, scale, or details.

Legend / disputed

A story reported by contemporaries or later writers that cannot be fully verified.
